Xtream Codes player for Android TV

Sign in with the server address, username and password from your provider and StreamX loads live TV, movies and series, with artwork and an automatic TV guide.

StreamX is a media player only. It does not provide any channels, movies, series or streams. You add a playlist, portal or login that you already have the right to use.

How to add an Xtream Codes login

  1. Open StreamX and open the Profile menu (your avatar, top right).
  2. Choose Manage Sources, then add a new source.
  3. Under Source Type, select Xtream Codes.
  4. Enter the Server URL exactly as your provider gave it, for example http://provider.tv:8080.
  5. Enter your Username and Password.
  6. Leave the EPG field blank to use the automatic guide that Xtream providers supply, or paste your own XMLTV link.
  7. Save, then sync. Live TV, Movies and Series fill in by themselves.

What you get

  • Live channels in the provider's categories, with favourites and search.
  • Movies and series with full metadata, seasons, episodes and Continue Watching.
  • An automatic TV guide where the provider offers one.
  • Stored on your device, with optional encrypted sync if you sign in to StreamX.

Tips

If a login does not work, check the server address for typos, make sure you include the port number, and confirm with your provider that the account is active. StreamX includes a connection test under Source tools in the Profile menu.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need for an Xtream Codes login?

The server URL, your username and your password, all supplied by your provider.

Does StreamX include any Xtream Codes accounts?

No. StreamX is a player only and includes no accounts, channels or content.

Can I test the connection?

Yes. Use Source tools in the Profile menu to test a connection and choose what syncs and when.
