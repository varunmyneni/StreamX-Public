Stalker and MAG portal player for Android TV

Use the portal address and MAC address from your provider and StreamX connects like a set-top box, with automatic session handling and quality selection.

StreamX is a media player only. It does not provide any channels, movies, series or streams. You add a playlist, portal or login that you already have the right to use.

How to add a MAG portal

  1. Open StreamX and open the Profile menu (your avatar, top right).
  2. Choose Manage Sources, then add a new source.
  3. Under Source Type, select MAG Portal.
  4. Enter the Portal URL, for example http://portal.tv/c/.
  5. Enter the MAC Address registered with your provider, in the form 00:1A:79:xx:xx:xx.
  6. Most portals work with the built-in defaults. Only if your portal requires specific device details, open Advanced and fill in the optional Device ID and related fields.
  7. Optionally add an EPG URL, then save and sync.

What you get

  • Live channels and video on demand from the portal, with the StreamX interface on top.
  • Automatic session handling, so you are not signed out in the middle of a programme.
  • Quality selection when the portal offers several streams.
  • Favourites, search, profiles and Continue Watching, the same as any other source.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Stalker or MAG portal?

A portal is an address your provider gives you that works with a MAC address, the way a set-top box does. StreamX can connect to it with the portal URL and MAC address.

Which details do I enter?

The Portal URL and the MAC address registered with your provider. A Device ID is optional and only needed for portals that require it.

Does StreamX provide a portal?

No. StreamX is a player only and includes no portals, channels or content.
